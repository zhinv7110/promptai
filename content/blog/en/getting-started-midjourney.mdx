## Why Prompts Matter

AI image generators like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and DALL-E are powerful tools, but they need direction. A well-crafted prompt is the difference between a mediocre result and a masterpiece.

Think of prompts as a conversation with the AI. The more specific and descriptive you are, the better it understands what you want.

## Basic Prompt Structure

A good prompt typically includes:

1. **Subject** — What or who is the main focus?
2. **Style** — Artistic style, medium, or aesthetic
3. **Lighting** — How the scene is lit
4. **Composition** — Camera angle, framing, focal length
5. **Quality** — Resolution, detail level, rendering quality

### Example Breakdown

**Basic prompt:**
```
a cat sitting on a windowsill
```

**Enhanced prompt:**
```
A fluffy orange tabby cat sleeping peacefully on a sunlit wooden windowsill, golden hour light streaming through lace curtains, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, cozy cottage atmosphere, photorealistic, 8K, editorial photography style
```

## Style Keywords That Work

- **Photorealistic**: 8K, hyperrealistic, sharp focus, detailed
- **Cinematic**: film grain, anamorphic lens, color graded
- **Anime**: Studio Ghibli inspired, cel shaded, 2D animation
- **Artistic**: oil painting, watercolor, sketch, concept art
- **Dark/Moody**: low key, dramatic shadows, noir

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Too vague** — "a beautiful landscape" tells the AI nothing
2. **Too complicated** — don't cram 50 adjectives into one prompt
3. **Ignoring negative prompts** — tell the AI what NOT to include
4. **Wrong aspect ratio** — always specify dimensions for your use case
