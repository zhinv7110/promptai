## Painting Styles

### Oil Painting
`oil painting, impasto texture, visible brushstrokes, classical art, rich pigments`
Best for: portraits, landscapes, historical scenes

### Watercolor
`watercolor, soft wash, flowing colors, paper texture, delicate`
Best for: botanical illustrations, dreamy landscapes, fashion

### Gouache
`gouache, opaque watercolor, flat color, matte finish, bold`
Best for: illustration, book covers, posters
